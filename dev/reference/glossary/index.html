    Glossary¶

    The language of running one kendo dojo: its members, their grades, the training sessions they attend, and the people who administer it.

    Language¶

    People¶

    Member: A person on the dojo's roster, with their personal details, grade and membership data. Holds the person's one email address, used both to contact them and to sign in. Avoid: Student, practitioner, kendoka, Member Profile, profile

    Admin: A Member whose Account has full management rights over members, sessions and configuration. Avoid: Sensei, manager, owner

    Visitor: A person who attends a Session without an Account, recorded by an Admin. Avoid: Guest, drop-in

    Coach: A Member flagged as having a coaching role within the dojo. Avoid: Instructor, teacher

    First Aider: A Member flagged as holding a first aid qualification.

    Authentication and identity¶

    Account: A Member's means of signing in: their Passkeys, recovery codes and Role. Not a person: it always belongs to exactly one Member. Avoid: User, login

    Role: The permission level of an Account: admin or member. Avoid: Permission, access level

    Passkey: A credential stored on a device, used to sign in without a password. Avoid: Biometric login, FIDO key

    Auth Session: One signed-in device: an Account and the Passkey it signed in with, until logout, expiry or revocation. Avoid: Session, login session, token

    First-registered Admin: The first Member on a fresh instance, whose Member and Account are created together when they claim it, with the admin Role. Avoid: Superuser, root user

    Membership¶

    Membership Status: A Member's current standing: active, inactive, suspended or archived. Active and inactive Members can sign in; suspended and archived ones cannot. Avoid: Account status

    Deactivation: Setting a Member's Membership Status to inactive. The Member is kept, not removed. Avoid: Delete, remove, archive

    Federation Number: A Member's identifier with a national kendo federation. Avoid: Licence number, membership number

    Federation Membership: A Member's registration with one federation: the federation, their Federation Number, and an optional renewal date. Avoid: Affiliation, federation link, member federation

    Emergency Contact: A person, not a Member, to be contacted on a Member's behalf in an emergency. Avoid: Next of kin

    Parental Consent: Confirmation that a parent or guardian has consented for a Minor to train. Avoid: Guardian consent

    Minor: A Member under 18, judged from their date of birth at the time it matters. Avoid: Under-18, child, junior

    Grading¶

    Grade: A Member's current rank in the standard kendo grading system. Avoid: Rank, level, belt

    Mukyū: The Grade of a Member who has not yet achieved 6-kyū. Avoid: Ungraded, beginner

    Kyū: A kyū-level Grade, from 6-kyū (lowest) to 1-kyū. Avoid: Kyu grade

    Dan: A dan-level Grade, from 1-dan to 8-dan (the highest in this system). Avoid: Dan grade

    Promotion: Advancing a Member from their current Grade to the next one. Avoid: Grading, upgrade, advancement

    Grade History: The ordered record of every Promotion a Member has received, with its date and the Admin who awarded it. Avoid: Grading history, grade log

    Sessions¶

    Session: A single scheduled training event at the dojo. Avoid: Class, training, practice

    Session Template: A recurring rule that generates Sessions on a fixed schedule. Avoid: Recurring session, schedule

    Ad-hoc Session: A one-off Session created directly, not from a Session Template. Avoid: Extra session, special session

    Segment: A timed block within a Session, with a start time and duration, carrying one or more Session Types. Avoid: Part, block, section

    Session Type: An admin-configured label describing the nature of a Segment, such as General Training or Grading. Avoid: Session category, class type

    Cancellation: Marking a specific Session as not taking place.

    Calendar Feed: The subscription of upcoming Sessions, without Cancellations, that anyone with its link can add to their own calendar app. Calendar apps show it under the dojo's name. Avoid: iCal, ICS feed

    Attendance¶

    Attendance: The record of who was present at a Session. Avoid: Register, roll call

    Check-in: A Member recording their own presence at a Session. Avoid: Sign-in, mark attendance

    Check-in Link: A Session's one public link for checking in. It grants nothing by itself: the Member still signs in, and the check-in window still applies. Avoid: Check-in token, sign-in code

    Admin Override: An Admin adding or removing a Member's Attendance after the fact. Avoid: Manual attendance, correction

    Attendance Rate: The share of held Sessions in a period that a Member attended, counting only from the later of the period's start and the day they joined. A Session under Cancellation is not held. Avoid: Consistency, attendance %

    Attendance Streak: A run of consecutive weeks, Monday to Sunday, in each of which a Member attended at least one Session. A week with no held Sessions neither breaks nor extends it. Avoid: Run, streak

    Absent Member: A Member with no Attendance in the last 14 days, including one who joined more than 14 days ago and has never attended. A state read from Attendance, not a Membership Status: nobody sets it, and it ends at their next Attendance. Avoid: Lapsed, inactive, missing

    Settings¶

    User Settings: The configuration every signed-in Member has for themselves: Personal Details and Appearance. Avoid: Settings, when Dojo Settings are also in scope

    Personal Details: The Member details a Member can edit themselves: name, date of birth, Emergency Contact and Federation Number. Avoid: Profile settings

    Appearance: The part of User Settings holding visual preferences, namely the Theme. Avoid: Display settings

    Theme: A Member's preferred colour scheme: light, dark, or system (following the device). Avoid: Dark mode, colour scheme

    Dojo Settings: Configuration for the whole dojo, managed by Admins: its name, primary colour and logo. Avoid: Settings, when User Settings are also in scope

    Deployment¶

    Environment names rather than domain concepts, pinned here because "production" was ambiguous between two stacks.

    Quick self-host: The single-file Compose setup of the app and its database. A supported production path for one dojo, and the one the install docs lead with. Avoid: Prod compose, docker prod

    Hardened deployment: The rootless Podman and Quadlet setup, with TLS termination, secrets and automated backups. What an operator graduates to from the Quick self-host. Avoid: The Quadlet thing, systemd deploy

    Release image: The published application container, pinned by its Release line. The app and nothing else: it holds no operator configuration. Avoid: The container, the GHCR image, the build

    Deployment bundle: The configuration a host needs beside the Release image, extracted from the image itself so the two are always on the same version. Avoid: The repo files, the deploy directory, the checkout

    Release line: The series of releases an operator pins to and upgrades within without a Breaking change: 0.3 before 1.0, 1 after. Only the latest Release line is supported. Avoid: Major, version, branch

    Version: The exact release an install is running, such as 0.1.5 or 0.2.0-rc.1; a build that is not a release is dev. One Release line holds many Versions. Avoid: Release line, build, tag

    Breaking change: A change an operator cannot take by the routine upgrade alone: it needs a manual step, or it cannot be undone by returning to the previous Release image. The REST API is not part of this promise; it ships with its only client. Avoid: Major change, incompatible change

    Relationships¶

    • Every Account belongs to exactly one Member; a Member has at most one Account. A Member has none until they redeem an invite (or, for the First-registered Admin, claim the instance), and none again after erasure.
    • An Account has exactly one Role.
    • Only an Admin can change a Member's email: the dojo cannot verify a self-service change.
    • A Member holds exactly one current Grade and accumulates a Grade History over time.
    • Grades progress in strict order: Mukyū, 6-kyū to 1-kyū, then 1-dan to 8-dan.
    • A Session is either generated from a Session Template or created as an Ad-hoc Session.
    • A Session has one or more Segments, and each Segment carries one or more Session Types, so one evening's keiko can be both Kihon and Keiko.
    • Attendance at a Session is a collection of Check-ins by Members and named Visitors.
    • A Session has exactly one Check-in Link, and it never changes.
    • A Minor needs Parental Consent before being fully registered.
    • A Deactivation never removes the Member or their Account.
    • When a Member becomes suspended or archived, every Auth Session they hold stops working.
    • A Member may hold a Federation Membership with each of several federations. An Admin manages any Member's; a Member manages their own.

    Flagged ambiguities¶

    • "User" is retired: it sounds like a person, and the person is always the Member. The sign-in is the Account; "the user's name" means "the Member's name". User Settings stays as a page name.
    • "Settings" alone covers both per-member preferences and dojo-wide configuration. Always say User Settings or Dojo Settings.
    • "Delete" is never the word for a Deactivation, in user-facing text, documentation or discussion, even where an operation is technically called delete.
    • "Profile" has meant the Account, the Member, or both. Say Member; avoid bare "profile" and the retired "Member Profile".
    • "Session" is a training event. Signed-in state is an Auth Session, always qualified.
    • "Grading" has meant both the event where grades are awarded and the act of awarding one. Say Session Type Grading for the event and Promotion for the act.
    • "Guest" and "Visitor" were used interchangeably. Say Visitor.
    • "Inactive" is a Membership Status someone sets. A Member who has stopped turning up is an Absent Member, never "inactive" or "lapsed".
    • "Rank" and "Grade": say Grade, in line with kendo federation terminology.
    • "Production" meant two different stacks, both supported production paths. Never use it to tell them apart: say Quick self-host or Hardened deployment.
